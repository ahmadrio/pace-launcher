# Tahap 10 (Date Differences, Quicklink Browsers, Deferred Fixes) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Day differences between two dates, a per-quicklink browser, honest time-zone abbreviations, and the tahap-9 deferred minors.

**Architecture:**
- Core:
  - `TimeZoneNames` (allowlist, offsets)
  - `DateQuery` (differences, leap days, offset labels)

  - quicklink opener with a browser and a fallback HUD
  - the "Open with" picker in Settings › Quicklinks

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6.4, SwiftPM, Foundation, AppKit (`NSWorkspace`), Swift Testing. Command Line Tools only.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-tahap-10-design.md`. Read it first, plus `HANDOFF.md` and the "Final: minor (deferred)" lines in `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-10-tahap-9-ledger.md`.

## Review Focus
1. **Labels match the clock.** `time in est` in July says UTC−4; `time in aest` in November says UTC+11; WIB stays "WIB". Test: Task 1 `` `abbreviations follow the city's clock` ``.
2. **Words are not zones.** `5pm to eat`, `cat`, `art`, `wet`, `may` → nil. Test: Task 1 `` `ordinary words are not zones` ``.
3. **Differences do not steal calculations.** `25 - 1` and `2025-1` stay calculator results, while `2026-12-25 - 2026-01-01` is a difference. Test: Task 2 `` `differences need two dates` ``.
4. **Leap days.** `days until 29 feb` → 2028-02-29; `days since 29 feb` → 2024-02-29. Test: Task 2 `` `29 february without a year` ``.
5. **A missing browser still opens the link.** The fallback opens the default and shows a HUD. Test: Task 4 UI check.

### Task 1: Time-zone abbreviations and cities (Core)

**Files:**
- Modify: `Sources/DOTLauncherCore/DateMath/TimeZoneNames.swift`, `DateQuery.swift`
- Test: `Tests/DOTLauncherCoreTests/DateQueryTests.swift`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `TimeZoneNames.zone(for:) -> (zone: TimeZone, place: String, showsOffset: Bool)?`. Abbreviations come from a fixed table of `abbreviation → (identifier, city)` as listed in the spec (CST → America/Chicago, IST → Asia/Kolkata, GST → Asia/Dubai, ICT → Asia/Bangkok, PHT → Asia/Manila, MYT → Asia/Kuala_Lumpur, HKT → Asia/Hong_Kong, MSK → Europe/Moscow, WET → Europe/Lisbon, EET/EEST → Europe/Athens, AKST → America/Anchorage, HST → Pacific/Honolulu, MT* → America/Denver, ACST/ACDT → Australia/Adelaide, AWST → Australia/Perth, NZ* → Pacific/Auckland). For these, `place` is the city and `showsOffset` is true. WIB/WITA/WIT/UTC/GMT keep their names with `showsOffset` false. Cities and aliases have `showsOffset` false.
  - `TimeZone(abbreviation:)` is no longer consulted.
  - New aliases: kolkata, bengaluru, bangalore, india → Asia/Kolkata.
  - `DateQuery` appends ` (UTC±h[:mm])` (U+2212 for minus; "UTC" alone for zero) to the place when `showsOffset`, computed at the result's instant.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests:**
  - `` `abbreviations follow the city's clock` ``:
    - with `now` = 2026-07-10 14:53 WIB, `time in est` → place "New York (UTC−4)"
    - with the November `now`, `time in est` → "New York (UTC−5)" and `time in aest` → "Sydney (UTC+11)"
    - `5pm in ist` → "Kolkata (UTC+5:30)"
    - `9:30am jst to wib` keeps place "WIB"
  - `` `ordinary words are not zones` ``: `zone(for:)` is nil for "eat", "cat", "art", "wet" and "may"; `evaluate("5pm to eat")` is nil.
  - `` `indian cities` ``: `kolkata`, `bengaluru` and `india` give a zone 19,800 s from GMT.
  - Update `` `zones by name` `` if needed (labels unchanged for wib/new york).
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `swift test --filter "DateQueryTests|TimeZoneQueryTests"`. Expected: failures.

### Task 2: Date differences and leap days (Core)

**Files:**
- Modify: `DateQuery.swift`
- Test: `DateQueryTests.swift`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `DateQuery.Result.difference(Int)` (absolute days).
  - Display: "228 days" / "1 day" / "Same day", copying the number.
  - Forms:
    - `<date> - <date>`: split on " - " (spaces required, so "2025-1" never splits)
    - `days between <date> and <date>`
    - `hari antara <date> dan <date>`
  - Year-less dates use the current year.
  - Countdowns step years (up to 8) until the day exists.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** (November `now`):
  - `` `differences between two dates` ``:
    - `17 aug - 1 jan` → 228
    - `1 jan - 17 aug` → 228
    - `2026-12-25 - today` → 45
    - `17 aug 2027 - 17 aug 2026` → 365
    - `days between 1 jan and 17 aug` → 228
    - `hari antara 1 januari dan 17 agustus` → 228
    - `today - today` → 0
    - display of `.difference(0)` → ("Same day", "0")
  - `` `differences need two dates` ``: nil for `25 - 1`, `2025-1`, `17 aug - bananas`, `17 aug -1 jan`, `days between 1 jan`.
  - `` `29 february without a year` ``: `days until 29 feb` → 476; `days since 29 feb` → 985.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `swift test --filter DateQueryTests`. Expected: failures.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement.** Arithmetic forms are tried first, so `today - 1 day` stays arithmetic.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run** `make test`. Expected: all pass.
- [ ] **Step 5: UI check** (test folders): `17 aug - 1 jan` shows "228 days"; `time in est` shows "(UTC−5)"; `25 - 1` shows the calculator's 24. App back on real data.
- [ ] **Step 6: Commit** `Count days between two dates`.
